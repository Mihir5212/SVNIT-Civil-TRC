About TRC

The consultancy and sponsored-research group of the transportation engineering faculty in the Department of Civil Engineering, SVNIT Surat.

Who we are

The TEP Research & Consultancy Cell (TRC) carries out consultancy and sponsored research for government departments, urban local bodies, traffic police and private organisations. The work is led by faculty of the transportation engineering group and supported by research scholars and project staff.

Being part of Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology, Surat means the agencies we work with get the institute’s survey equipment, laboratories and research experience, together with the independence of an academic organisation.

What we aim for

Safer roads and better-run traffic systems in Gujarat and across India, built on careful measurement and sound engineering. Every study should end with clear findings that our partner agencies can defend and act on.

Our capabilities

Studies and advisory work for government agencies, urban local bodies, traffic police and private developers.

How a project runs

  1. Enquiry

    You tell us the location, the problem and any deadlines, through the enquiry form, email or a letter.

  2. Scope and proposal

    We visit the site if needed and send a proposal with the scope of work, methods, timeline and fees as per institute norms.

  3. Field work and analysis

    Surveys are carried out and the data is analysed using standard and research-grade methods.

  4. Report and handover

    We submit the report with drawings or data as agreed, and present the findings to your team.

Facilities and tools

Studies draw on the survey equipment and software available in the department, including:

  • Video cameras and drone footage for traffic data collection
  • Automatic traffic counters and speed measurement devices
  • GPS units for travel time and delay surveys
  • Traffic simulation and analysis software
  • GIS tools for crash mapping and network analysis
  • Computer-vision tools for vehicle detection and tracking

Working with the institute

Consultancy projects are taken up through the institute’s consultancy process, with agreements, invoices and reports issued on behalf of SVNIT Surat.
