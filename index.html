    Why agencies work with TRC

    Impartial by design

    As part of a national institute, we have no stake in contracts or construction. Our reviews and recommendations rest on the data alone.

    Measured, not assumed

    Our studies start in the field, with video-based surveys, counters and crash records, so recommendations reflect how traffic actually behaves on Indian roads.

    Research methods, usable outputs

    Faculty and research scholars apply current methods, then deliver reports, drawings and dashboards that engineers and officers can act on.
