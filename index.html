About TEP

The TEP Research & Consultancy Cell (TRC) carries out consultancy and sponsored research for government departments, urban local bodies, traffic police and private organisations.

Being part of SVNIT Surat gives the agencies we work with the institute’s survey equipment, laboratories and research experience, with the independence of an academic organisation.

More about us

Vision & Mission

Vision

Safer roads and better-run traffic systems in Gujarat and across India, built on careful measurement and sound engineering.

Mission

To end every study with clear findings that our partner agencies can defend and act on.
